Questions

EV Charger Installation FAQ

The questions we hear most from GTA homeowners before booking an install.

How much does EV charger installation cost?Most Asked

Cost depends on your panel capacity, charger type, and the distance from your panel to the install location. Use the estimator above for a starting range, or call for a free, no-obligation quote based on your specific setup.

How long does an installation take?

Most residential installations are completed in a single visit, typically a few hours, depending on whether a panel upgrade is also needed.

Do I need a permit for EV charger installation?

Yes — an ESA permit and inspection is required in Ontario. We handle the permit and inspection for you as part of every install.

Which EV charger brands do you install?

We install all major EV charging brands, including Tesla Wall Connector, ChargePoint, and JuiceBox, for both residential and commercial properties.

Do I need a panel upgrade for an EV charger?

Not always — it depends on your home's existing electrical capacity. We perform a load calculation during the quote to confirm if an upgrade is needed.

Can I install a charger in a condo or townhouse?

Often yes, though condos may require property management or board approval first. We can assess your parking setup and advise what's realistic before you apply.

Still have questions? Talk to a licensed electrician directly — no hold music, no call center.
