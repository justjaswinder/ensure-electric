EV Charger Installation FAQ

How much does EV charger installation cost?

Cost depends on your panel capacity, charger type, and distance from your panel to the install location. Call us for a free, no-obligation quote based on your specific setup.

How long does an EV charger installation take?

Most residential EV charger installations are completed in a single visit, typically a few hours, depending on whether a panel upgrade is also needed.

Which EV charger brands do you install?

We install all major EV charging brands, including Tesla Wall Connector, ChargePoint, and JuiceBox, for both residential and commercial properties.

Do I need a panel upgrade for an EV charger?

Not always — it depends on your home's existing electrical capacity. We assess your panel during the quote to confirm if an upgrade is needed.
